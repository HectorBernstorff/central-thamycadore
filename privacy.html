Privacy Policy

This is the privacy policy of Central Thamy Cadore, the private personal assistant of Thamy Cadore (the "owner"). Only the owner uses the assistant, to organize her own work. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data controller: Thamy Cadore. Contact: contato@thamycadore.com.

What data we collect

  • WhatsApp Business. With the owner's authorization, through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform, the assistant receives the messages sent and received on her business numbers, up to 180 days of one-to-one chat history (groups are not included) and the contact list of those numbers (name and phone number). This includes messages from people who talk to the owner.
  • The owner's Google accounts. Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Docs, Sheets and Contacts, through Google APIs.
  • Conversations with the assistant. What the owner writes or says to the assistant, including through Telegram, the channel she uses to talk to it.

We do not collect browsing data from visitors to this site.

How and why we use the data

The data is used only to help the owner with her own work: summarizing and finding conversations, reminding her of open items and appointments, organizing tasks and drafting replies.

  • No message is sent on the owner's behalf without her confirmation.
  • The assistant does not contact the people who talk to the owner on its own.
  • The data is not sold, not used for advertising and not used by us to train artificial intelligence models.

Who the data is shared with

The data is stored on a private computer, with access restricted to the assistant and to the person who administers the system for the owner. To work, the assistant uses these services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform), which delivers the messages;
  • Google, which holds the owner's email, calendar and files;
  • Anthropic (Claude), the artificial intelligence service the owner subscribes to, which reads the data needed at the moment she makes a request;
  • Telegram, through which the owner talks to the assistant.

Other than that, data only reaches another person when the owner asks for an action that involves them, such as replying to a message. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How long we keep the data

The data is kept while the owner uses the assistant. When she disconnects an account, the assistant stops receiving its data and the stored data is deleted.

How to request deletion of your data

Anyone, including people who talked to the owner on WhatsApp, can request deletion of their own data. Email contato@thamycadore.com with the subject "Data deletion" and include the phone number or email address used in the conversations. We reply from the same address confirming the deletion. See also the data deletion instructions.

The owner can also disconnect WhatsApp Business from the app on her phone and revoke access to her Google accounts at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Your rights

Under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (Law No. 13,709/2018), you may request confirmation that we process your data, and access, correction and deletion of it, at the email address above.
